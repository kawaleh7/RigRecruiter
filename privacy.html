Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

RigPass Inc. ("RigPass", "we", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

From Workers:

  • Name, email address, and password
  • Province, trade, and years of experience
  • Safety certifications including issue and expiry dates
  • Uploaded resume documents
  • Profile visibility preferences

From Employers:

  • Company name, email address, and contact information
  • Billing information (processed securely via Stripe)
  • Search activity and shortlist data

Automatically collected:

  • IP address and browser type
  • Pages visited and time spent on platform
  • Device and operating system information

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and improve the RigPass platform
  • To match workers with relevant job opportunities
  • To send certification expiry alerts and platform notifications
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions
  • To prevent fraud and ensure platform security

3. Who Can See Your Profile

Workers control their profile visibility. You can set your profile to:

  • Public — visible to all verified employers on the platform
  • Employer-only — visible only to paid employer accounts
  • Hidden — not visible to any employer

We never share your personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with:

  • Stripe — for payment processing
  • Email service providers — for sending alerts and notifications
  • Cloud hosting providers — for platform infrastructure
  • Law enforcement — only when required by law

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data is removed within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

6. Your Rights

Under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request corrections to inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@rigpass.com.

7. Security

We use industry-standard encryption and security practices to protect your data. All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.

8. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you logged in and maintain your session. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes by email or in-app notice.

10. Governing Law

This policy is governed by the laws of the Province of Alberta, Canada, and complies with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).
