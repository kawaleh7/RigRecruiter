About RigPass

Built in Alberta.
Built for the patch.

RigPass was founded by people who've worked in the oil and gas industry and lived the problem firsthand — chasing down expired safety tickets, losing placements over paperwork, and watching good workers miss out on jobs because their certs weren't organized.

We built the platform we wished existed — one that keeps workers compliant, makes employers confident, and gets the right people on site faster. Simple as that.
